Connected Accounts
******************

Connected accounts let us sign in to our FAIR Wizard account through a configured external identity provider.

Connect an account
==================

#. Sign in to the FAIR Wizard account we want to keep using.
#. Open :guilabel:`User settings` → :guilabel:`Connected Accounts`.
#. Under :guilabel:`Connect Account`, select the configured provider.
#. Complete the provider's authentication flow, then check that the connection appears in the list.

Available providers are configured by the instance administrator. If the identity is already linked to a different FAIR Wizard account, contact the administrator to resolve it.

Disconnect an account
=====================

Choose :guilabel:`Disconnect` beside the connection and confirm. We can no longer use that connection to sign in to this account. Before disconnecting it, make sure we have another working sign-in method.
