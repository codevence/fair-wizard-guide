Workspaces
**********

A workspace contains projects, members, user groups, knowledge models and document templates for a team or department.
An organization can provide shared knowledge models and document templates through its library.

With one workspace, we can access projects and content directly. With multiple workspaces, we first select the workspace whose projects and content we want to use.

Our account has an organization role and can have a role in each workspace we belong to. Permissions from the organization role may also apply within workspaces.
For managing membership, see :doc:`/administration/workspaces`; for shared and local content, see :doc:`/knowledge-models/index`.
