FAIR Wizard Guide
*****************

.. image:: /_static/illustrations/success.png
   :alt: FAIR Wizard penguin holding a check mark.
   :width: 120
   :align: right

In FAIR Wizard 5.0, we can work on projects, create knowledge models and document templates, and manage our organization. The available features depend on our role and permissions.

The guide describes both single-workspace and multi-workspace instances. Differences are explained on the relevant pages.
