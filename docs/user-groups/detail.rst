Manage a User Group
*******************

Open a group to review its details and members. Group owners can change its name, description and visibility, and add or remove members. Users with **Manage User Groups** can manage groups within their permitted scope.

Choose a member's group role to make them an owner or an ordinary member. Save your changes. Group ownership controls the group itself; access to a project depends on the role assigned when the group is added to :doc:`project sharing </projects/detail/sharing>`.

.. admonition:: Multiple workspaces

   Add people who are already members of the group's workspace. Groups cannot span workspaces. Removing someone from a workspace also removes their membership in its local groups.
