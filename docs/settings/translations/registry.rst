Official translations
*********************

The translation list provides access to available official translations. Choose the application or email category before installing or updating a language.
Review the installed version and enable the language after installation where necessary.

Official updates do not overwrite a language you have replaced with a custom PO file, and do not automatically restore a language you deleted.
Use :doc:`upload` when you intend to maintain a custom translation.
