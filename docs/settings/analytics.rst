Analytics synchronization
*************************

The organization's Analytics settings configure synchronization of application data into the reporting database.
We can check the last synchronization and its status, and configure regular synchronization.
The scheduled time uses our local time zone. Save changes after setting or changing the schedule.
Large imports or content changes may require a new synchronization before Analytics reflects them.

With multiple workspaces, reporting contains workspace information, while the synchronization settings remain organization-wide.
Use :doc:`/analytics/index` for views, filters and exports within Analytics.
