Settings
********

Settings are divided into Users & Access, Projects, Appearance, Features & Integrations, Translations, and Plan & Usage. The available settings depend on our permissions and the services enabled in the instance.

Organization defaults and workspace overrides
=============================================

In single-workspace mode, manage the organization's settings directly.

.. admonition:: With multiple workspaces

   Workspaces use the organization's settings by default. If the organization allows it, we can configure a settings section for a workspace. Resetting it restores the organization's settings.
   This replaces the whole section, not just individual fields.

Supported workspace sections include projects, dashboard and menu, support, document submission and permitted plugin settings.
Authentication, login screen, organization-wide appearance, features, translations and plugin enablement are managed at organization level.
